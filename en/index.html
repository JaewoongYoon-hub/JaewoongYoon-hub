AboutProfile

Build it fast, then make it stick in the organization

I started at POSCO DX in 2008 and have worked across ERP, data analysis, RPA and visualization in the POSCO Group ever since. At POSCO Energy (now part of POSCO International) I worked on a company-wide process diagnosis, then joined the PI task force it led to, owning the plant maintenance and quality modules in the move from Oracle ERP to SAP ERP. I then ran SAP ERP for five years, including authorizations and month-end closing issues, and separated the IT systems of the Korea Fuel Cell spin-off. More recently I led the PMO for an overseas subsidiary's ERP rollout and the ERP selection for another.

Today I plan and deliver company-wide DX, and I spread data analysis, automation and visualization skills through a course program I designed myself.

When someone in the business says "I wish we had a tool for this," I use generative AI and vibe coding to show a working prototype within minutes. I aim to be the person who builds the solution and gets it adopted, not just the one who explains the technology.

PPCC 2026: Power Platform moves from app tool to agent operating model

Power Platform Community Conference 2026 · Oct 27–29, MGM Grand, Las Vegas · 9,000+ attendees

The message this year: apps, automation, agents, code and governance now form one operating model. The edge is shifting from building fast to running agents that work safely.

Year over year · 2M+ monthly active makers and 4M+ apps (Microsoft)

  1. Agents become coworkersCopilot Studio agents act under their own accounts in Microsoft 365, start on triggers and remember business processes. The Microsoft Copilot harness runs longer, complex work.
  2. The line between building and coding blursApps in Copilot Studio (public preview), app creation in Copilot Cowork, natural-language canvas and model-driven apps. Developers pick up the same solution in VS Code, GitHub Copilot and Copilot CLI.
  3. AI that understands the businessBusiness applications in Work IQ (public preview), plus Dataverse semantic models, Ask API, Business Skills and MCP Server.
  4. Code under governanceMicrosoft Copilot Managed Runtime hosts code created with Copilot under enterprise governance.
  5. Governance and spend in one placeMicrosoft Agent 365 gives one view of all apps and agents, action-level connector policies and spending controls by user and asset.

Customer stories · Asurion raised touchless invoice processing from under 20% to over 70% with a Copilot Studio agent; the NFL saves 8 to 12 hours a week of data entry and reporting.

Data

Build
Shared ALM for makers and developers; standards for MCP and GitHub Copilot.
Run
An owner, approver and stop rule for every agent, with a regular inventory review.
Cost
Measure credit use against business results; set limits and alerts per user and agent.

Most features are in preview, with no GA dates or usage limits announced. Start small: assess (0–4 weeks), design (1–2 months), pilot two or three high-value processes (2–4 months), then scale (4–9 months).

ResearchMBA thesis

MBA thesis · 2023.08 · Ajou University Graduate School of Business

The impact of nuclear power expansion on Korea's System Marginal Price, forecast with deep learning

Shows that the generation mix chosen in the national power supply plan moves the wholesale electricity price (SMP). Holding settlement prices at 2021 levels and applying the 10th Basic Plan's generation mix, an LSTM model forecasts the 2030 SMP.

−22.2%

Forecast 2030 SMP versus the 2021 actual. LSTM validation MAPE 8.6%, against 13.8% for RNN.
